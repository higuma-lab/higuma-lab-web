This policy explains how NoteFlip (the "app"), published by HIGUMA LAB, handles your information.

## Summary

NoteFlip does not collect, send or share any personal information. It has no account, no advertising and no analytics, and it does not connect to the internet. Everything you put into the app stays on your PC.

## What the app stores on your PC

The app keeps the following in its own storage folder on your PC (the app's local data folder, which Windows manages separately for each app):

- **Your scores**: copies of the PDF files you import. The original files are not changed.
- **Your handwriting** on each page, and the **folders and set lists** you make.
- **Settings**: the display language, your page turn keys, the colors and widths of the pencil and highlighter, the score and page you last had open, and when a trial reminder was last shown.
- **Cache**: thumbnails of first pages and an index of your library, to start faster.
- **A diagnostic log** (up to about 5 MB; the oldest entries are removed automatically). It records what the app did and any errors, so that problems can be investigated. It does **not** contain the names of your scores, files, folders or set lists, or your search words; scores are recorded only by a random ID, and folder paths are masked.

None of this is sent anywhere by the app. When you uninstall NoteFlip, Windows deletes all of it. PDF files you export and library backups you make are saved where you choose and are not deleted. If you save a backup to a cloud service such as OneDrive, that service's terms apply to it; the app itself never sends your backup anywhere.

## Sharing the log with us

The log stays on your PC. If you ask us for help, you may choose to send it to us: open the "…" menu and select **Open log folder**. We use a log you send only to investigate your problem.

## Microsoft Store and Windows

NoteFlip is offered through the Microsoft Store. Purchases, the free trial and licenses are handled by Microsoft; we do not receive your payment details. See the [Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement).

Depending on your Windows settings, Windows may send crash and diagnostic data to Microsoft. Microsoft may share summaries of crashes of the app with us through its developer portal (for example, where in the app a crash happened). This data does not identify you to us.

## Contacting us

If you email us, we use your email address and what you send only to reply to you, and we delete it when it is no longer needed.

## Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
